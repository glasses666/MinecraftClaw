# Riverside Hut Candidate

This blueprint is tuned for a riverbank or steep river-adjacent slope, not a flat meadow lot.

## Shape

- Small raised footprint with stone-brick footings and a narrow front deck.
- Main body is shifted back from the water-facing edge so the house feels seated on the slope.
- The roof is stepped instead of flat, with a lighter ridge and a dark overhang to read well in rain.

## Environmental Intent

- The front deck faces the open side of the site and can read as a lookout over water.
- The stone base is meant to visually anchor the hut into a wet, uneven terrain.
- Lanterns and the campfire are placed to make the house feel warm in low-light weather.

## Risks

- If the site is too narrow, the front deck and support columns may collide with existing terrain.
- The roof overhang is intentionally pronounced, so placement needs enough lateral clearance.
- The build is still template-based; it adapts better to slope than to highly irregular cliff faces.
