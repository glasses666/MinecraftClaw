# Lookout Atelier Candidate

This candidate is a river-facing lookout atelier for a steep stone slope or cliffside shelf.

Design intent:
- Keep the public face open toward water or a broad vista.
- Anchor the rear into the slope with a heavier cobblestone/service volume.
- Use a split mass: a lighter front lookout wing and a taller rear workshop core.
- Avoid the flat, rectangular "box house" look by biasing the roof and materials.

Material language:
- `stone_bricks` for the base and contact with terrain.
- `spruce_planks` for the entry terrace and sheltered lower edge.
- `oak_planks` for the main studio shell.
- `dark_oak_planks` and `dark_oak_slab` for the upper mass and asymmetrical roof.
- `glass_pane` bands to create a studio / atelier feel instead of a closed cabin.

Functional layout:
- Front terrace and lookout band.
- Central working room with crafting table, chest, furnace, and shelves.
- Rear service mass that can read as chimney / storage / hill anchor.
- Warm lantern lighting and a single bed for a lived-in feel.

Environment assumptions:
- Best when the open view is on the front side of the blueprint.
- Better on a terrace or natural ledge than on a fully flat plain.
- The current planner does not rotate blueprints, so placement orientation matters.

Main risks:
- If the site is too flat, the asymmetry may read weaker than intended.
- If the open view is not at the front edge, the lookout wing points the wrong way.
- The rear cobblestone mass can feel heavy if the surrounding terrain is already visually dense.
- This is still a fixed-step blueprint, not a runtime-generated shape, so it is a candidate rather than a fully parameterized system.
